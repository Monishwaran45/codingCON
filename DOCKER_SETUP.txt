TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: "Port 27017 already in use"
Solution: Change port in docker-compose.yml
  mongodb:
    ports:
      - "27018:27017"  # Change to 27018

Problem: Docker not installed
Solution: Download from https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop

Problem: Services won't start
Solution: Check logs
  docker-compose logs

Problem: Backend can't connect to services
Solution: Ensure docker-compose is running
  docker-compose ps
