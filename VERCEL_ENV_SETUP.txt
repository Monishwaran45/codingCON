            HOW TO ADD ENVIRONMENT VARIABLE IN VERCEL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Go to your Vercel project
  https://vercel.com/dashboard

STEP 2: Select "codingcon-frontend"

STEP 3: Click "Settings" (top menu)

STEP 4: Click "Environment Variables" (left sidebar)

STEP 5: You'll see the Environments screen
  - Production (tracked to main branch)
  - Preview (other branches)
  - Development (local)

STEP 6: Click on "Production" to edit it

STEP 7: Click "Edit" or "Add Variable"

STEP 8: Add the environment variable:
  
  Key:   NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL
  Value: https://codingcon-backend-01b9.onrender.com
  
  Click "Save"

STEP 9: Go back to "Deployments"
  - Find latest deployment
  - Click "Redeploy"
  - Wait 1-2 minutes for rebuild

STEP 10: Done!
  Your frontend will now connect to your backend
